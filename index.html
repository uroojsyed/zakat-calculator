i
Amounts due soon

Track eligible short-term amounts that are currently due, such as bills, rent, invoices or salaries. Do not include the full balance of a long-term loan.

Watch the Human Appeal video explanation
Total amounts due:

Often included: cash, savings, cryptocurrency, gold and silver jewellery, shares and accessible pension contributions, business stock, and debts you expect to recover.

Often deducted: unpaid rent, house payments, utilities, property payments, invoices, staff salaries, and other short-term debts that are due.

These are general educational notes, not a fatwa. Religious opinions can vary — please consult a trusted scholar for guidance on your circumstances.
